Cyclio Support

Cyclio is a local-first cycling dashboard and ride recorder for Android.

Get help

Email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL. Include the Cyclio version, Android version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Do not send backup passwords. Before sharing screenshots, GPX files, or backup files, check that they do not reveal private locations or other information you do not want to disclose.

Quick checks

  • For missing or inaccurate ride data, confirm that precise location and GPS are enabled.
  • For interrupted recording, review Android battery restrictions and notification permission for Cyclio.
  • Before reinstalling the app or clearing its data, create a manual backup.
  • Passwords for encrypted backups cannot be recovered.
